# Router Domain Shift Pareto

The authoritative counts are generated in
`evaluation_results/ROUTER_V4_DOMAIN_SHIFT/report.json`; document-level opaque
IDs and categories are in `failures.json`. Categories are derived from frozen
evidence in order: identity, weighted anchors, geometry zones, structure,
service table, eligibility and margin. Custom and non-claim failures retain
separate categories. No representative text or image content is persisted.
