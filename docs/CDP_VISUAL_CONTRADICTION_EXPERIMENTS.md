# Visual Contradiction Experiments

VC-01 measured the frozen visual baseline. VC-02 added structural and service-table contradiction. VC-03 added opposing-standard evidence. VC-04 added semantic-anchor and spatial-geometry contradiction. VC-05 added paired margin/entropy ambiguity. VC-02 through VC-05 had identical recall and false-standard outcomes on the independent safety corpus, demonstrating that later evidence families did not recover the large third-source generalization failure.

Per the stop rule, further classifier stacking, retraining, and frozen-suite tuning are prohibited for this cycle. The evidence indicates source representation/taxonomy mismatch rather than inadequate fusion capacity.
